# Capsule Ecology — the Ecology Arc (product direction)

Presented by Nathan 2026-11-02 13:36 EST as "Capsule Ecology Roadmap:
From Prototype → Ecosystem → Civilization Platform" (Phases 0–9),
immediately after his sequencing call: **3D-textured world first,
then identity.** Filed as the product-direction arc for the identity
work. It does NOT replace ROADMAP.md (R0–R9, the engineering build
order) — the R-phases are how; this arc is what they add up to.
One build line stands: R0–R9.

## The arc (condensed from his text)

- **Phase 0 — Foundation (current).** Ledger, contracts, gate,
  manifests, hash verification, toolbox, restore engine, Bible,
  voxel globe prototype. Success: verify + reverse actions, agents
  under governance, receipts = trustable history.
- **Phase 1 — The Living Dashboard.** Ecology metrics as living
  visualization: Population (citizens, not "agent count"), guild
  count, ecosystem health, task flow, entropy score, receipt rate.
  Cockpit UI; status understood in 5 seconds.
- **Phase 2 — Agent Genetics.** DNA traits (Curiosity, Analysis,
  Memory, Creativity, Discipline, Governance), scored; birth events
  inherit traits/memories/capabilities/contracts; evolution tracking
  (parent, mutation, generation, survival score).
- **Phase 3 — Ecosystem Physics.** Mass = importance, Velocity =
  rate of change, Momentum = continued focus, Gravity = idea
  attraction, Friction = resistance, Entropy = knowledge decay.
  Users watch concepts orbit, projects merge, ideas drift.
- **Phase 4 — Guilds and Civilization.** Research / Creator /
  Engineer guilds with memories, reputation, achievements,
  receipts, ancestry.
- **Phase 5 — The Living World.** The globe IS the interface:
  Knowledge→Forests, Authority→Mountains, Activity→Rivers,
  Models→Cities, Receipts→Roads, Conflicts→Storms, Archives→Ruins,
  Governance→Capitals. Terrain/biome/weather simulation, settlement
  growth, world history + replay.
- **Phase 6 — Capsule Exchange.** .capsule contains World, Agents,
  Guilds, Receipts, Memories, Contracts, Models, Settings.
  Import/export, verification, lineage preservation, marketplace.
- **Phase 7 — Emergence.** Weekly Ecology Reports (new lineages,
  guild growth, entropy deltas, retirements, rename PROPOSALS at the
  Gate), historical timeline + replay.
- **Phase 8 — Capsule OS.** Browser, toolbox, agents fuse: unified
  search/memory/governance/receipts/worlds/identity.
- **Phase 9 — Civilization Network.** Cross-capsule diplomacy,
  shared projects, receipt federation, ancestry preservation,
  guild alliances.

North star (his text): "A persistent digital civilization where
people, agents, knowledge, worlds, receipts, and governance evolve
together over years, while preserving ancestry and user authority."
